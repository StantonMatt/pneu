How it works

Pair

Install Pneu on your phone and computer. On the computer, choose Add device and scan the QR code with your phone.

Computers can pair with each other too.

Connect

Works on your home Wi-Fi. Away from home, it connects through Tailscale.

Send

  • New screenshots go to your computers automaticallyPhotos and screen recordings too, if you want
  • Copy on one computer, paste on another
  • Send your phone’s clipboard with one tap
  • Share any file to one device or all of them

Privacy

Your devices talk directly to each other, encrypted. No account, no server, and no data collected. Privacy policy
