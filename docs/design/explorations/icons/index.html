Picks

Crossing

The only concept that shows what Pneu promises, instant arrival, rather than how the tube works. It keeps the shipped indigo and amber, so the v2 palette and screens stay as they are.

Two flat fields and one shape stay crisp from 256 px down to the 16 px glyph, and the ghost-to-solid capsule could become the Link's sending animation.

Pip

The one people would remember. A character suits a personal app, its silhouette and eyes still read at 24 px, and Pip could appear in the Link, empty states and pairing.

Two things to settle first: the orange tile breaks the “only indigo is coloured” rule (Pip can sit on the indigo tile instead), and the white-and-blue capsule should be checked for a pill read.
