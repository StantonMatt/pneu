Design v2 · approved

Pneu, redesigned

One indigo, one mark, and one signature view: the Link from your phone to your PC. Every feature, setting and flow from v1 stays. What changes is hierarchy, colour, shape and motion, on both the phone and Plasma.

Indigo means live

One brand colour, #4F5BFF, for the connection and primary actions. Amber is paused, red is broken, grey is offline. Nothing else is coloured.

The Link

Home and the PC window lead with the connection itself. A capsule runs along it while something is sending.

Native, not stock

System font, One UI shapes, edge-to-edge and true-black dark mode, with Pneu's own tokens instead of wallpaper colours.

Quiet by default

No taglines or helper text. A second line appears only for a reason, a path, a count or a time.

Icon

Crossing

Phone and PC are two fields. The carrier is still a ghost on the phone side and solid where it has landed: instant arrival, not the tube. Chosen in the second icon round (all concepts), it replaces the tube P everywhere.

Full asset set

Design system

Tokens and components

Fixed palette (no dynamic colour). Light and dark values side by side. Dark mode is true black with lifted cards, which suits the S25+ OLED panel and matches One UI.

Colour

Type

System font (One UI Sans on the phone, Noto Sans on Plasma). Sizes in sp. Mockups use Inter as a stand-in.

The Link

The one custom component. Endpoints are phone and PC; the tube between them carries the state. Animated here as on the device (respecting reduced motion).

Components

Phone

Every screen and state

412 dp frames (Galaxy S25+), light and dark. Click any phone to see it at full size.

PC · KDE Plasma 6.6

Window, tray and notifications

The window is drawn with Pneu's components and follows Plasma's light or dark scheme. The tray menu and notifications are drawn by Plasma: Pneu controls the icon, structure and words.

Window

400 px wide, height fits. Closing hides it; Esc closes it. Title-bar ✕ replaces the old Close button.

Tray menu and tooltip

Structure and labels unchanged from v1. Checkmarks and highlight are Plasma's own.

Notifications

Copy unchanged from v1. The new pneu icon appears in the header; file previews use x-kde-urls.

Versus v1

What changes

  • Colour. A fixed indigo palette replaces wallpaper-based dynamic colour. Status colours become semantic: indigo live, amber paused or needs attention, red broken, grey offline.
  • Icon. New mark across launcher, themed icon, notification glyph, both tiles, PC app icon and tray.
  • Home. The status disc becomes the Link. Buttons are unchanged; Resume and Pair again are the only filled buttons. Sending progress moves from a ring around Cancel to a bar under the row. Don't send any is red.
  • Banners. Amber on a neutral card instead of a red slab; the local-queue banner stays red.
  • Settings. The paired PC moves to the top as an identity card with Unpair. Clipboard moves above Connection. Section headers are grey, not accent-coloured. Switches lose the check icon.
  • Setup. The app icon sits above the title on first run. Rows get icon tiles; done rows get an indigo check.
  • Pair. Corner brackets instead of a full outline. "Pair with PC" is no longer drawn (it stays the TalkBack pane title). Success fills the frame.
  • Dialogs. Bottom-anchored floating sheets (One UI's placement) with two equal buttons; destructive confirms are red.
  • PC window. Pneu components instead of stock Breeze widgets; the Close button goes (✕ and Esc remain); Unpair… moves into the card as a red row; the status dot follows the phone's colours.
  • Tray. New glyph. Disconnected is the glyph at 50 %; receiving turns it indigo; paused and error keep Plasma's badges, now top-right.
  • Unchanged. Every setting, toggle, default, flow, rule and string, except the hidden Pair title.

Decided

  1. Icon. Crossing, from the second icon round: a dashed ghost on the phone side, solid amber where the carrier has landed. Replaces the tube P.
  2. Palette. Fixed Pneu palette, no dynamic colour.
  3. PC window. Pneu components, following Plasma light/dark.
  4. Close button. Removed; title-bar ✕ and Esc close the window.
  5. Font. System fonts on both sides.
